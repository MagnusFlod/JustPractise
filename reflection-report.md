I started with creating a new area for my project. I chose to use the Scrum and corporation-model. 
The area is simply called, Semester-Project. This area will include Epics, user-stories, tasks, a timeline, backlog and so on.

To get things started I chose to create 4 Epics for this project. They are called: 
Make table for receptionist, Track Deliveries, Make Digital Clock Program 
and Style with Company Brand.

I made these 4 Epics in consideration of the main customer requirements. 

Screenshot of the 4 Epics ![alt text](<Skjermbilde 2025-11-25 103650.png>) 

In the first Epic, I added 3 user-stories. The goal of the user-stories are: Make a table for staff-members. 
Make In and Out buttons. Make a toast-warning if a staff-member is late. 
I tried to keep in mind that the user is the receptionist and he/she is the one who is going to use this table in their everyday worklife. 
So it's important that the user-criteria is met.

I made 2 sprints. They are called: Table and deliveries Sprint. Date, time and CSS Sprint. 
The Table and deliveries Sprint is the biggest sprint for this project.

Table and Deliveries Sprint is the largest sprint and that's why I've decided to give it 3 weeks in time. 
I was originally going to make 3 sprints. Table and deliveries Sprint. Date and Time Sprint. CSS Sprint. 
I do know that sprints usually have a time-span of one to two weeks, but I saw that implementing Table and Deliveries-logic 
would take up most of the time of this project, while 
Date and Time would take maximum one day. Thats why I made the (maby unconventional) choise to extend the time for Table and Deliveries
and combine CSS, Date and time.
